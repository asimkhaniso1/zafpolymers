Who we are

A privately owned vinyl-bag manufacturer built for export.

Established in 2005, ZAF Polymers is a privately owned manufacturer and exporter of vinyl bags based in the Karachi Export Processing Zone, Pakistan. From a 4,000-square-yard facility staffed by over 200 people, we produce packaging for home textiles, apparel and retail across a wide range of PVC and environmentally friendly materials.

Our factory is an approved, audited supplier for major international retailers, holding compliance IDs across Walmart, Target, JC Penney, K-Mart and Revman — and certified to the Global Recycled Standard.
